Flow-through

Year-by-year cap structure

Revenue × growth → Revenue. Revenue × EBITDA margin → EBITDA. EBITDA × FCF conversion → FCF. FCF allocated to buybacks / dividends / debt paydown; remainder builds cash. Net debt = total debt − cash carries to next year.
